# PRODUCT.md

## Register

`brand` — this site IS the product. Recruiter/hiring-manager-facing portfolio. Design quality is itself a signal.

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Muhammad Hussain Ahmad, ML/AI & Robotics Engineer (4 years experience, NUST). Acts as a credible single-page resume that a recruiter or engineering manager can read in 60 seconds and immediately know: who he is, what he ships, whether to schedule a call.

## Users

- **Primary**: technical recruiters and ML/robotics hiring managers screening candidates. Skim first 5 seconds. Read carefully if signal is strong.
- **Secondary**: peer engineers and collaborators. Want to see actual code, project depth, technical taste.
- **Tertiary**: future Hussain. Reference page for sharing in cold outreach, LinkedIn bio link.

## Strategic Principles

- **Substance over polish-for-polish-sake.** Reader should leave knowing what he can build, not how clever the site looks.
- **Academic gravitas.** Serif typography, generous whitespace, no startup-pitch energy. The page should feel like a researcher's homepage even though the content targets industry hiring.
- **Quiet confidence.** No metrics theater ("$5M saved"), no "AI co-founder" pitch language. Show, don't shout.
- **Scannable.** Hiring managers skim. Strong hierarchy, short paragraphs, clear sections.

## Tone

Plain, considered, slightly formal. Closer to an academic CV than a SaaS landing. First-person but restrained. No exclamation points, no emojis, no marketing voice.

## Anti-references

Avoid:
- Dark "AI startup" aesthetic (gradient blobs, neon, animated mesh)
- Bento-grid feature cards
- "Building the future of X" copy
- Hero stats / kpi blocks
- Dense icon walls / "tech stack as logo grid"
- Modal dialogs, scroll-jacked sections
- The previous version of this site (sidefolio dark / "AI co-founder" framing)

## References

- Andrej Karpathy (karpathy.ai) — serif body, sectional rhythm
- Lilian Weng (lilianweng.github.io) — essay-first typography
- Classic CS prof pages with Times New Roman bodies
